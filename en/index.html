FAQ

Good to know

Do I need an appointment?
No. Everything happens online: you compose your invitation and see the result live.
Can my guests read it in English?
Yes. Each plan includes two or three languages, and more can be added.
Does the price depend on the number of guests?
No. Each plan is a single payment per wedding, whatever the number of guests.
Is my website private?
Personal links open directly for each guest, and you can protect the general page with an access code.
Can I change my mind?
Yes, within the 14-day withdrawal period described in our terms of sale.

The ordering space is currently in French; write to us from your space if you need help in English.
